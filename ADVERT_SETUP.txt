TripBuddy V1.4 - Existing Companion adverts.json format

Your supplied adverts.json is already in advertising/adverts.json.
Copy the image files referenced by its 'image' fields into the images/ folder, retaining the exact filenames, capitals, spaces and extensions.
Example: images/Klook Mediterranean Adventures Banner.png
Upload the contents of this ZIP to your TripBuddy GitHub repository, including advertising/ and images/.
The banner rotates every 8 seconds, using rotationSeconds in adverts.json.
The cruise-only advert is excluded from TripBuddy; target=all adverts are shown.
No 1.5MB image limit is enforced in TripBuddy. Large images may take longer to load.
Affiliate URLs are taken directly from your adverts.json.

Note: This ZIP does not contain your banner images; copy them from your existing Companion app repository.
